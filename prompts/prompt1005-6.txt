==================================================
2. CORE SCIENTIFIC QUESTION FOR GATE 2
==================================================

The intended Gate 2 question is:

Among cities with similar present-day urban states and comparable large-scale environmental contexts, do cities with different developmental histories also show systematically different urban thermal responses?

Conceptually:

S = present-day urban state
H = developmental history
C = large-scale environmental context
R = urban thermal response

We ultimately want to test whether:

R contains information associated with H beyond S and C,

without making unsupported causal claims.

This is observational global comparative science.

Do NOT frame the design as:

"history causes heat"

or:

"urban history has a causal effect on SUHI"

unless a future design provides causal identification, which is not currently authorized.
