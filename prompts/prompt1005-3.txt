2. CURRENT CANDIDATE SCIENTIFIC QUESTION
======================================================================

The current candidate scientific question is:

"Does the pathway by which a city formed leave a measurable
environmental legacy after its present-day state and natural background
are accounted for?"

A provisional statistical expression is:

H not independent of R conditional on S and C

where:

S = present-day urban state
H = historical urban development pathway
C = background environmental/climatic context
R = environmental response

This is a CANDIDATE framing.

Do not convert it into a finalized hypothesis.

======================================================================
3. THREE CANDIDATE SCIENTIFIC TESTS
======================================================================

Audit feasibility for three candidate tests.

TEST A — INCREMENTAL HISTORICAL INFORMATION

Baseline:

M0:
R ~ S + C

History-aware:

M1:
R ~ S + C + H

Question:

Does adding H improve strictly out-of-sample explanatory/predictive
performance after S and C are controlled?

Do not fit these models in this execution.

--------------------------------------------------

TEST B — PRESENT-DAY URBAN TWINS

Candidate city pairs satisfy approximately:

D_S(i,j) small
D_C(i,j) small

while:

D_H(i,j) large

Question:

Does environmental-response divergence D_R increase with historical
divergence D_H among present-day analogues?

Do not identify actual city pairs in this execution.

--------------------------------------------------

TEST C — HISTORY-AWARE TRANSFERABILITY

Compare analogue selection based on:

A0 = analogue(S,C)

versus:

A1 = analogue(S,C,H)

Question:

Does history-aware analogue selection reduce out-of-sample transfer
error?

Do not run this experiment.

15. IDENTIFIABILITY / CIRCULARITY RED-TEAM
======================================================================

This is a critical section.

Actively try to invalidate the proposed study.

Create:

results/1005-3/identifiability_risk_register.csv

At minimum inspect:

A. Mathematical coupling

Does H mathematically contain or reconstruct S?

B. Outcome leakage

Does H contain information derived from R or a close proxy?

C. Temporal leakage

Are future observations used to define historical predictors?

D. Boundary leakage

Does a 2020 boundary determine historical measurements in a way that
creates artificial relationships?

E. Spatial autocorrelation

Could nearby cities make cross-validation appear better than true
transferability?

F. Climate confounding

Could history merely proxy climate zone or latitude?

G. Development-level confounding

Could history merely proxy income/development stage?

H. Urban-size confounding

Could city age simply proxy city size?

I. Sensor/product artifacts

Could apparent history effects arise because old/dense urban cores have
different retrieval properties?

J. Rural-reference contamination

Could SUHI differences be caused by inconsistent rural reference areas?

K. Survivor/selection bias

Does the urban-centre dataset preferentially include cities that reached
a modern threshold?

L. Modifiable areal unit problem

Could results depend strongly on city boundary definition?

M. Multicollinearity

Can S and H be statistically distinguished?

N. Post-treatment control

Could controlling for present-day morphology erase the pathway through
which history actually acts?

For every risk provide:

- mechanism
- severity
- detectability
- proposed falsification/sensitivity test
- whether it is fatal or manageable

Use severity:

LOW
MEDIUM
HIGH
CRITICAL
